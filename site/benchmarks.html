Evaluation

Anchor vs mini-swe-agent,
on the official grader.

Same task images, same model, same runner, same patch extraction, same SWE-bench grader. The only variable is the harness.

Method

An A/B that isolates the design.

The setup

One deterministic subset of SWE-bench Lite — 10 tasks, one per repo — booted from the official task images (amd64, emulated on Apple Silicon: exactly the images the benchmark numbers come from). Both arms run inside that container; patches are extracted by the same runner; resolution is decided by the official swebench grader.

The baseline

mini-swe-agent is embedded through its Python bindings, in the same container as its counterpart — its battle-tested SWE-bench template, non-interactive mode. None of mini's own environment management is in play, so the comparison measures agent design, not tooling.

The accounting

Anchor's tokens and dollars are exact — provider usage priced from live catalog rates. mini's tokens are estimated (serialized history ÷ 4) and priced from that estimate, because litellm has no pricing entry for the round's model. Disclosed, not hidden.

The same 7/10, with a third of the bill.

  • Same resolution, ~3.3× cheaper and ~3.2× leaner tokens. The score is the price of entry; the ledger is the point. Anchor spends its context effort on memory (notes, spill, compaction from the real window, cached prompts) instead of re-serializing everything it has ever seen.
  • Where the two diverged, it was 1–1. matplotlib-18869 was resolved by anchor and left unresolved by mini; seaborn-2848 went the other way. flask-4045 and sympy-11400 were unresolved for both — no hidden gimmes on either side.
  • mini is often quicker in wall-clock; anchor is often deeper in work. On pylint-5859 and xarray-3364 anchor took long, deliberate paths — and resolved both. Wall time is the trade: emulated containers and thorough verification against 3× smaller bills.
  • Receipts included. Every anchor run ships a replayable trace of the whole episode (typed events, redaction applied before persistence) and tree-hash evidence for its test runs. The benchmark reuses production mechanics — nothing is special-cased for the eval.
